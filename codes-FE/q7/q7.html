Step-by-Step Tutorial

Follow the instructions below to make tender chicken:

(in under 40 mins)


  1. Wash the chicken
  2. Prepare the aromatics
  3. Warm up the oil (on a high flame) on a pan or anything
  4. Put the chicken pieces onto the oil
  5. Let the pieces form crust underneath the surface
  6. Time to put the aromatics and flip the pieces
  7. Put gas or induction to medium setting and cover with lid for slow cook
  8. Ocassionaly stir the pieces so that it doesn't get burned
  9. After it is cooked, let it sit for about 10 mins
  10. Enjoy Your Chicken !
